## Write your introduction here

Lead with the reader's problem and a short answer. Include the main local
keyword once in the first paragraph. Reference the business name and town
naturally (e.g. "TBR Groups in Marthandam").

### Sub-heading (H3, keyword-rich)

Break the article into scannable sections. Each H3 should answer one
question the reader asked.

- Bullet lists make steps easy to scan.
- Use plain, factual language.
- Keep sentences short.

1. Numbered lists work well for step-by-step instructions.
2. One step per item.

> Tip: pull out a key takeaway as a blockquote for emphasis.

## Call to action

End with a clear next step and a local mention — e.g. "Call TBR Groups
today to get it fixed, or drop by any of our four branches in
Kanyakumari District."
